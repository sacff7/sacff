About SACFF

Our mission

To have a God’s family of believers experiencing a life-changing, personal, and intimate relationship with Jesus through exaltation, edification and evangelism.

We are families from across San Antonio who gather on Saturdays through the month — to pray in one another’s homes, to open the Scriptures together, and to worship. You are welcome to attend SACFF services; come and worship Him in spirit and in truth.

  • Exaltation — a spirit-filled, non-denominational worship service.
  • Edification — the Word studied chapter by chapter, verse by verse.
  • Evangelism — guest preachers and evangelists from India and across San Antonio.

Our Founders

San Antonio Christian Family Fellowship was founded in 2001 by two families who began meeting simply to pray together. Twenty-five years on, the fellowship still gathers around the same four things — prayer, the Word, worship and fellowship.

Dr. Ravindranath Duggirala

Founding Member

One of the two founding members of San Antonio Christian Family Fellowship, Dr. Ravindranath Duggirala helped bring the first families together for prayer in 2001. What began as a small circle in a living room has grown into a fellowship spanning generations, still resting on the foundation it started with — prayer, and the Word at the centre of every gathering.

Dr. Rector Arya

Founding Member

A founding member alongside Dr. Duggirala, Dr. Rector Arya has shared in the life of the fellowship since its very first gatherings in 2001 — in prayer, in the study of Scripture, and in the conviction that families grow strongest in faith when they grow together. Across more than two decades that welcome has extended to every generation who has joined us.
